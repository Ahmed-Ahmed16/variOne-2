## Root cause (confirmed, not theory)
- NRF + TFT + CC1101 all share **GPIO 11(MOSI)/12(SCK)/13(MISO)**. Every Arduino pin→SPI assignment — `SPIClass::begin()`, `spiAttach*()`, **and `pinMode()`** — routes through the ESP32 **peripheral manager** (`perimanSetPinBus`/`perimanClearPinBus`), which runs the previous owner's deinit → `spiStopBus()` → **tears the bus down**. NRF grabbing a shared pin silently destroyed the TFT's SPI2 → screen freeze; a later lgfx draw into the dead bus deref'd garbage (`0xff000000`) → the serial crash. Verified by reading `esp32-hal-periman.c` + matching the two bus addresses (`0x3fca1544` SPI3 vs `0x3fca1534` SPI2) in serial logs.

## Key invariants — DO NOT regress
- **NEVER call `pinMode` / `spiAttach*` / `SPIClass::begin()` on GPIO 11/12/13 after boot.** All three trigger the periman teardown that kills the TFT (or NRF) bus. Use **raw GPIO matrix + `gpio_set_direction` only** — [nrf_common.cpp `nrf_claimBus`/`nrf_releaseBusToDisplay`](src/modules/NRF24/nrf_common.cpp) and the `nrf_needsBusRemux` branch in `nrf_start`.
- **SPI3 must be started on the throwaway pins `NRF_SPI3_DUMMY_SCK=1` / `DUMMY_MOSI=21`** ([nrf_common.cpp](src/modules/NRF24/nrf_common.cpp)). `begin()` on the real pins re-breaks everything. (If a board ever wires 1/21, pick other free GPIOs.)
- The re-mux helpers are gated by `nrf_needsBusRemux` (set only in the CC1101-shared branch) — no-op on other boards. Callers must wrap NRF-read/TX vs TFT-draw phases.
- Prior invariants still hold: NRF rides SPI3, CC1101 stays on `tft.getSPIinstance()` (SPI2), SubGHz saves → LittleFS.
