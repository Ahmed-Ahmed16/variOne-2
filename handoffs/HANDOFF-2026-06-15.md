## Root cause (confirmed, not theory)
- **`CC1101_bus.mosi == TFT_MOSI` (both GPIO11)** → `initRfModule()` routes CC1101 to **`tft.getSPIinstance()`** ([rf_utils.cpp:222](src/modules/rf/rf_utils.cpp#L222)) = the LovyanGFX bus on **SPI2_HOST**. That's why CC1101 coexists with the TFT.
- NRF was routed to `CC_NRF_SPI` = **FSPI = SPI2_HOST too** (`USE_HSPI_PORT` → `#define VSPI FSPI`, [main.cpp:32](src/main.cpp#L32)). A 2nd Arduino `SPIClass` driver fighting LovyanGFX on the **same SPI2 peripheral** → every NRF transfer spun forever (verified: probe C never printed on the FSPI build; `tft.drawPixel` also hung).
- Fix = give NRF its **own SPI3/HSPI** peripheral (free on this board). TFT_SPI_HOST=SPI2, CC_NRF=FSPI/SPI2, SPI3 unused → NRF claims it. Real MISO=13.
- Confirmed harmless: the `addApbChangeCallback(): duplicate` log — `spiStartBus` ignores its return ([esp32-hal-spi.c:859](file:///home/ahmed/.platformio/packages/framework-arduinoespressif32/cores/esp32/esp32-hal-spi.c#L859)) and still sets the clock. Red herring.

## Key invariants — DO NOT regress
- **NRF24 MUST ride its own `static SPIClass nrfHSPI(HSPI)` (SPI3), never `CC_NRF_SPI`/FSPI/SPI2.** SPI2 is the TFT's host; sharing it = transfer spin/freeze. See [nrf_common.cpp:78](src/modules/NRF24/nrf_common.cpp#L78).
- **CC1101 stays on `tft.getSPIinstance()` (SPI2) — untouched.** Do not reroute it.
- NRF and TFT **share physical GPIO 11/12** on *different* peripherals (SPI3 vs SPI2). LovyanGFX routes its pins **only at bus init** ([Bus_SPI.cpp:196](.pio/libdeps/varione-s3-sd-smoke/LovyanGFX/src/lgfx/v1/platforms/esp32/Bus_SPI.cpp#L196)), NOT per transaction. So every NRF↔TFT handoff needs an explicit GPIO re-mux (see Open).
